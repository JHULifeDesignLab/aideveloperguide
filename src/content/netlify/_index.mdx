## About
Netlify is one of the two canonical "push your frontend, get a URL" platforms (the other being [Vercel](/aideveloperguide/vercel)). Connect a GitHub repository — or literally drag a folder onto the browser — and your site is live on a global CDN with HTTPS. It popularized this whole workflow, and it remains one of the friendliest on-ramps to deployment that exists.

This guide is deliberately shorter than our Vercel one: the concepts transfer almost entirely, so we focus on what's distinct — Netlify's famously simple deploys, its built-in extras like forms and redirects, and the judgment call of when to pick which platform.

## What you'll accomplish

- **Deploy two ways** — the drag-and-drop instant deploy and the git-connected workflow with previews
- **Use the built-in extras** — redirects for single-page apps, environment variables, forms without a backend, and serverless functions
- **Develop platform judgment** — free-tier boundaries and an honest Netlify vs. Vercel vs. Render decision rule

## Why Netlify specifically?

Two honest reasons. First, the drag-and-drop deploy is the single fastest path from "folder on my laptop" to "URL I can text someone" — a genuinely great first-deploy experience. Second, employers don't care *which* of these platforms you used; they care that you understand the workflow (build → preview → promote) and can explain your choice. Knowing both major players makes that explanation credible.
