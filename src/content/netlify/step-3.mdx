## Goal
Know the free tier's real boundaries, and finish with the three-platform decision rule that makes any deployment choice defensible in an interview.

---

## The free tier, honestly

Netlify's free plan comfortably hosts portfolios, class projects, and demos: a monthly allowance of bandwidth, build minutes, and function invocations. The specific numbers move — check [current pricing](https://www.netlify.com/pricing/) — but the practical boundaries are stable:

- **Build minutes are the one students actually hit.** Every push triggers a build; a slow build × many pushes adds up. If you're iterating hard, batch pushes or build locally and drag-drop while prototyping.
- **Functions have execution limits** — same serverless shape as everywhere: fine for API calls, wrong for long-running work.
- **Overage behavior matters:** know whether hitting a limit pauses the site or bills you, and configure accordingly. Check the pricing page rather than assuming.

Employers reading "deployed on Netlify" on a resume aren't impressed by the noun — they're listening for the workflow vocabulary (previews, redirects, build settings, environment separation) and the reasoning. You now have both.
