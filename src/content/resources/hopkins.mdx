## Hopkins Career Support
</div>

Everything in this guide pairs with real, in-person support on campus. These two university resources are the best places to take your next step — whether that's planning a job search or getting a second pair of eyes on your resume.

### Tech & Analytics Pathway

The **Blue Jay Tech & Analytics Pathway** is the Imagine Center's hub for students exploring careers in software engineering, data science, and analytics. Its Tech Playbook collects recruiting timelines, application strategy, and skill-building resources specific to tech roles — the hiring-process context that pairs well with the technical skills this guide teaches.

### Life Design Lab — Career Guidance

The **Life Design Lab** (the team that provides this guide) offers one-on-one career guidance for JHU students: resume reviews, interview practice, internship search strategy, and help figuring out which direction fits you. Stop by drop-in hours to talk with a life design educator — no appointment needed. Current contact information and drop-in hours are listed on the staff page.
